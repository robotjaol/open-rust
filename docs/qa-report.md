## Defects found during QA and fixed

1. Secondary buttons rendered their label in white on light surfaces (explicit `Color32::WHITE` galley colour). Fixed with `Color32::PLACEHOLDER`.
2. Pages were wider than the window because `set_max_width(1180)` enlarged the layout region. Fixed with `min(available_width)`.
3. Status bar text was clipped at the bottom edge. Fixed with explicit centred layout.
4. Cards inside `ui.columns` justified their text. Fixed by forcing a top-down, left-aligned layout in `card`.
5. Dashboard framerate and welcome toggle label issues noted in screenshots were corrected.

## Known open issues

- Right-to-left layouts and non-Latin input were not tested.
- The Welcome page toggle has a visible label next to it; other toggles rely on the row title.
- Window-state restore was not exercised on a multi-monitor setup.
